## Method and limits

- Same high effort label, 1200-second window and 256KiB decoded source ceiling do not imply equal internal computation or provider response-token limits.
- Claude correction schema attempts capped at 1; no fallback, paid API, extra artistic pass or coordinator drawing edit.
- CLI cost estimates are list-price estimates; actual subscription charges are unexposed and recorded as null.
- Missing artwork is a completion result under this protocol, not an artistic-quality score.
- Technical coordinator QA was unblinded; frozen aesthetic review used a fresh reviewer with neutral images and anonymous continuity bundles. AI review is not human acceptance.
- Eight outer CLI author launches are recorded, not eight provider response attempts. Fable draft and correction each hit an output-token ceiling and automatically continued internally; neither produced a completed package before the shared wall-time limit. The schema-validation cap does not suppress this separate CLI continuation behavior. Exact internal response counts are not exposed.
