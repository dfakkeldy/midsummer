# Historical source and redistribution presentation

The base is the 1863 Cambridge edition, Volume II, edited by William George Clark (1821–1878) and William Aldis Wright (1831–1914); William Shakespeare died in 1616. The underlying historical contributions are used as public-domain source material in the project’s USA/Canada context. This is a rights determination for the identified historical material, not a universal claim about unrelated modern editions.
