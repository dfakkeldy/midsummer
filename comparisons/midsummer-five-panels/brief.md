# Five bright Midsummer paintings

Create five original unlettered still paintings, P01–P05, as one independent code-painted package. Each is 1920 × 1080 and must tell its scene clearly at 360 pixels wide. These are illustrative stagings, not claims that each pose occurs literally in Shakespeare.

Bright and colourful is mandatory: jewel-magenta, sapphire, emerald, turquoise, violet and gold; vivid flowers; luminous forest light. Use Rackham-inspired fine expressive ink contours, organic branching forms, transparent watercolour, pigment texture and delicate crosshatching. Keep clothing colours rich and figures distinct from their surroundings. Night scenes remain colourful and readable. Avoid a muddy sepia result, anime conventions, cartoon mascot faces, generic stick figures, faceless mannequins and geometric paper-doll limbs. Draw original mature human faces and anatomy, with graceful flowing contour shapes and deliberate fingers, joints and cloth folds.

All characters are original unmistakably adult, awake and unenchanted. Glamour comes from elegant fitted opaque gowns, covered dramatic necklines, bare shoulders, tasteful skirt slits, confident poses and expressive acting. Cover breasts, buttocks and genitals. No nudity, sexual acts, underwear reveals, intimate body-part emphasis, voyeuristic low angles, childlike designs or sexualized sleeping/magically controlled characters. Show whole poses, faces, action and relationships.

## Fixed scenes

- P01 — Root throne: Awake, unenchanted Titania sits on a mossy root with knees crossed, one hand braced on the seat and the other resting naturally. She wears a jewel-magenta wrap gown amid vivid flowers. Show the full pose at a normal eye-level angle. Test seated anatomy, hands, cloth folds and contact with the seat. Make the crossed knees unmistakable; natural joined hips, thigh/knee/calf transitions, two feet and physical support. Confident elegant glamour, full-body staging.
- P02 — Forest stride: Adult Helena steps over a root in a bright jewel-toned gown; its outer skirt and her hair catch a breeze. The fabric suggests movement while preserving coverage. Test weight, foreshortening, action silhouette and fabric. Clearly show one planted supporting foot and one lifted stepping foot, a credible pelvis/torso balance, two distinct legs and breeze direction.
- P03 — Lily-pool portrait: Adult Hermia turns toward an off-frame voice beside a pool. Bright lilies and luminous reflected light frame a medium portrait with a readable expression. Test adult face, expression, hands and delicate watercolour. The mature face is the focal point, three-quarter turn, eyes aimed toward the unseen speaker, a subtle raised brow and attentive parted lips; one natural hand may rest at her collarbone or on the pool bank.
- P04 — Moonlit disagreement: Awake, unenchanted Helena and Hermia face one another across a fallen branch. Their distinct costumes, gestures and expressions carry the disagreement; colourful moonlight keeps both readable. Test two-character staging, eyelines, occlusion and character continuity. Show adult tension through poses and faces, open conversational gestures; neither figure covers the other's face. Keep enough figure size for phone readability.
- P05 — Canopy court: Awake, unenchanted Titania holds court among enormous roots and brilliant flowers while an adult Puck watches from a branch. Keep Titania large enough to read within the elaborate forest. Test organic environment, scale, lighting, detail and focal hierarchy. A vast asymmetrical twisting canopy, layered roots, jewel flowers and luminous clearing frame the queen. Puck is clearly a mature adult, secondary in the branch, never a tiny childlike fairy.

Titania remains recognizable between P01 and P05; Helena between P02 and P04; Hermia between P03 and P04. Follow cast.json. Every scene has its own deliberate composition. Use large readable figure silhouettes and uncluttered faces. Keep heads, hands, meaningful feet, crossed knees and important props inside a 5% safe margin. No text, speech bubbles, labels, captions, models' names, watermark, audio or animation. Background branches may extend to the edges.

## Deliverable and budget

Return only a JSON object with schema_version: 1 and files: [{path, content}, ...]. Exactly three JavaScript files: src/lib/palette.js, src/lib/cast.js, src/scenes/panels.js. No Markdown wrapper. Decoded source must total at most 256 KiB; aim for a complete, detailed but efficient 18–45 KiB package rather than exhausting a provider's response ceiling. Palette and drawing helpers are your own original work. Do not reuse another lane's implementation.

Use only the supplied frozen p5/p5.brush painted engine. Do not edit engine files or redeclare its globals PAL, W, H, TAU, PROJECT, LOOPS, lerp, hash, etc.; choose your own prefix. Drawing calls are paint, inkLine and approved geometry/light helpers; transforms push/pop/translate/rotate/scale and p5 seeded random/noise are allowed. Do not use clawd, anime/cel rigs, cel, plain p5 rect/ellipse/line shapes, canvas/SVG drawing or external raster images. Draw original humans with organic polygons, curves and painted details. Do not fetch/import/require external assets, network, filesystem, DOM, eval, timers, dynamic functions or Math.random. No tools, external lookup, subagents, rendering or visual self-revision. Return the entire initial package in one response. The coordinator renders it once and may send one correction packet, with the same allowance for every model.

Register LOOPS.P01 through LOOPS.P05, each a function(t) with len=1, painting the whole still frame at t=0. At the start of EACH loop call randomSeed(424242); noiseSeed(424242); after the engine's reset. Other deterministic hash helpers are allowed. Every loop must be independent of prior calls. Avoid thousands of fill shapes: reserve true watercolour fills for broad backgrounds, clothing shadows and focal areas, use opaque wash for skin/base clothes/small flowers plus fine ink. Group repeated same-colour marks. A 90-second external renderer timeout applies equally to every panel. The common engine uses brush.scaleBrushes(5): inkfine at sw approximately 0.15–0.5 makes delicate native contours, while sw=1 is substantially thicker. Use colours and texture to carry volume, not heavy black rings.
