# Never Ran Smooth

[Verse 1]
Athens. A wedding in four days,
Theseus and Hippolyta.
Egeus drags his daughter in:
Hermia, take Demetrius.
She won't. She loves Lysander.
So the duke reads out the law:
die, or wear a nun's veil all your life.
Then the lovers plan to run
through the wood to his aunt's,
where Athens' law can't follow.

[Verse 2]
Helena loves Demetrius;
he wants Hermia instead.
She tells him of the flight
for one kind word.
He hunts the lovers;
she hunts him.
And Quince calls out his workmen:
Bottom gets Pyramus.
They'll rehearse their tragedy
for the duke's wedding night
deep in the same dark wood.

[Chorus]
Never ran smooth, never ran smooth,
true love was never going to run smooth.
Through the wood, through the fog, through the night,
it never ran smooth, but we ran.

[Verse 3]
The wood. A war of fairies.
Oberon wants a boy
Titania raises for his mother,
her friend who died.
She won't let him go.
Their quarrel drowns the fields,
frost lands on summer roses.
Oberon sends Puck for a flower:
drip it on sleeping eyes,
they'll love the first live thing they see.

[Verse 4]
He hears Demetrius drive Helena off:
Puck, charm that Athenian.
He streaks his sleeping queen himself.
Puck finds Athenian clothes,
Lysander, not Demetrius.
Wrong eyes. Helena wakes him;
he swears he loves her now
and leaves Hermia asleep.
Helena calls it mockery and flees.
Hermia wakes to an empty wood.

[Chorus]
Never ran smooth, never ran smooth,
true love was never going to run smooth.
Through the wood, through the fog, through the night,
it never ran smooth, but we ran.

[Verse 5]
Puck plants a donkey's head on Bottom.
His friends scream and scatter.
Titania wakes, enchanted,
and loves him; fairies serve him.
Oberon sees the error,
charms Demetrius himself.
Two men vowing love to Helena;
she's sure it's a cruel joke.
Hermia, robbed and called a puppet,
turns on her oldest friend.
The rivals leave to fight.

[Bridge]
Fog. Black fog.
Puck throws each rival's voice,
leads them apart in circles
till they drop. Four asleep.
Cure on Lysander's eyes.
Only his.
Demetrius stays enchanted.

[Verse 6]
Oberon says the charmed queen gave up the boy.
He frees her eyes.
Puck lifts the donkey head;
Bottom's own face returns.
The fairies make their peace.
Dawn. Horns. Theseus finds four asleep,
overrules Egeus: three weddings, his and theirs.
Lysander, cured, is Hermia's again.
Demetrius, still under the flower,
loves Helena.
Bottom wakes, can't say his dream,
runs back to his crew.

[Verse 7]
Night. Three couples, wed, watch the play.
A talking Wall. A Lion stains Thisbe's cloak;
Pyramus thinks she's dead,
stabs himself; she does the same.
Only actors falling.
Bottom sits up. The court laughs.
Two players dance; then midnight.

[Final Chorus]
Never ran smooth, never ran smooth,
true love was never going to run smooth.
Through the wood, through the fog, through the night,
it never ran smooth, but we ran.

[Outro]
Fairies bless the house.
Puck says, if we offended,
call it a dream, and pardon us.
Give us your hands.
It never ran smooth, but we ran.
