# If We Shadows

[Verse 1]
Four days out, Theseus and Hippolyta,
wedding on the way.
Egeus wants Demetrius for Hermia;
she has to obey,
or she dies, or she lives as a nun.
She loves Lysander, so they plan to run:
tomorrow night, through the wood,
to his aunt's house, gone for good.

[Verse 2]
Helena loves Demetrius,
but he wants Hermia.
She tells him they're running, hoping for thanks;
he follows them, she follows him,
chase, chase, into the dark.
Same night, Quince's crew, Bottom as Pyramus,
rehearse a wedding play,
Pyramus and Thisbe, deep in the wood.

[Chorus]
If we shadows have offended,
think you slept, and all is mended.
Call it a dream, call it a dream,
but who's still enchanted?

[Verse 3]
Fairy king, fairy queen,
fighting for a boy,
her dead friend's son; she keeps him close.
Floods and frost, the seasons lost.
Oberon wants him. Puck, fetch the flower:
juice on sleeping eyes,
love the first live thing you spy.
Demetrius spurns Helena. Puck, charm that man.

[Verse 4]
Oberon charms his sleeping queen.
Puck sees Athenian clothes. Wrong man, wrong man:
Lysander wakes to Helena,
swears he loves her, leaves Hermia sleeping.
Helena thinks it's mockery and runs;
Hermia wakes alone, alone, calling out his name.

[Chorus]
If we shadows have offended,
think you slept, and all is mended.
Call it a dream, call it a dream,
but who's still enchanted?

[Verse 5]
Donkey head on Bottom. Crew runs, crew runs.
Queen wakes, charmed, loves him; fairies serve.
Oberon sees the slip, charms Demetrius himself.
Two men chase Helena; she thinks it's a joke.
Hermia, betrayed. Rivals go to duel.
Puck drops fog, throws their voices,
round and round till four fall down.

[Bridge]
Cure on Lysander's eyes. Only his.
Demetrius wakes still wearing the flower.
Oberon says the charmed queen gave up the boy;
he frees her, Puck lifts the donkey head
off Bottom's own, and the fairies dance.

[Verse 6]
Dawn horns. Theseus finds them, overrules Egeus;
three couples will wed, his own among them.
Lysander, cured, back with Hermia;
Demetrius, still enchanted, with Helena.
Demetrius says some power changed his heart.
Bottom wakes, can't say his dream,
runs back to his crew.

[Chorus]
If we shadows have offended,
think you slept, and all is mended.
Call it a dream, call it a dream,
but who's still enchanted?

[Verse 7]
Wedding night, three couples watch:
Wall in the middle; Lion stains Thisbe's cloak;
Pyramus thinks her dead,
stabs himself; she does the same. Just actors.
Bottom gets up. Court laughs. Two players dance; midnight.
Fairies bless the house.

[Final Chorus]
If we shadows have offended,
think you slept, and all is mended.
Call it a dream, call it a dream,
but who's still enchanted?
If you pardon, we will mend it.
Give me your hands, and we'll be friends.

[Outro]
Puck sweeps the dust behind the door.
Good night unto you all.
Call it a dream, call it a dream.
Who's still enchanted?
