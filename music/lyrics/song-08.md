# Ill Met by Moonlight

[Verse 1]
Ladies and gentlemen, Athens, four days out:
Theseus weds Hippolyta, the whole town shouts.
Then Egeus storms in with his daughter in hand:
Hermia weds Demetrius, that's my command!
She loves Lysander (oh no), so the duke lays down the law:
death, or a nun's life, that's all.
So the lovers plan to run tomorrow night
through the wood to his aunt's, out of Athens' sight.

[Verse 2]
Helena loves Demetrius; he won't love her back,
he wants Hermia. So she spills the plan for thanks.
He chases the lovers, she chases him.
Then Quince's crew of working men,
Bottom the weaver strutting as Pyramus,
heads for the duke's oak to rehearse a wedding show:
Pyramus and Thisbe. (Here we go!)

[Chorus]
Ill met by moonlight! (Ill met!)
Everybody's lost tonight.
Ill met by moonlight! (Ill met!)
Wake up wrong and love on sight.
Ill met, ill met by moonlight.

[Verse 3]
In the wood the fairy king and queen are throwing down
over a boy she keeps for her friend who died.
Rivers flood, the seasons flip. Oberon wants that child.
He sends Puck for a flower: juice on sleeping eyes,
you love the first live thing you spy.
He sees Demetrius dump Helena: Puck, charm that man!

[Verse 4]
Oberon drips it on the sleeping queen.
Puck finds Athenian clothes on a sleeper. Wrong man! (Wrong man!)
Lysander wakes to Helena, swears he's hers,
leaves Hermia sleeping. Helena cries mockery and splits;
Hermia wakes alone in the dark.

[Chorus]
Ill met by moonlight! (Ill met!)
Everybody's lost tonight.
Ill met by moonlight! (Ill met!)
Wake up wrong and love on sight.
Ill met, ill met by moonlight.

[Bridge]
Now here comes Bottom, rehearsing near the queen.
Puck gives him a donkey head! (Hee-haw!)
His crew runs screaming; Bottom sings to show he's brave.
Titania wakes, enchanted: what angel's that?
Fairies scratch his ears and fetch him honey.
(Let him roar again! Let him roar again!)

[Verse 5]
Oberon spots the slip and charms Demetrius himself;
two men chase Helena, and she says it's a prank.
Hermia feels robbed; the rivals go to duel.
Puck rolls the fog, throws their voices, leads them round
till all four drop. Cure on Lysander's eyes, his alone;
Demetrius stays charmed, and nobody knows.

[Verse 6]
Oberon says the queen, still charmed, gave up the boy;
he frees her eyes, Puck pulls the donkey head off Bottom,
his own face fine, and the fairy pair make peace.
Morning horns: Theseus overrules Egeus.
Lysander, cured, is Hermia's; Demetrius, still charmed, is Helena's;
three couples will wed, the duke's included. Bottom wakes,
can't explain the dream, runs back to the crew. (He's back!)

[Verse 7]
Wedding night, three couples in the hall:
a Wall, a Moon, a Lion stains Thisbe's cloak.
Pyramus thinks she's dead and stabs himself; she does the same.
It's only acting, y'all! Bottom pops back up,
the court cracks wise, and two players dance.

[Final Chorus]
Ill met by moonlight! (Ill met!)
Everybody's lost tonight.
Ill met by moonlight! (Ill met!)
Wake up wrong and love on sight.
Ill met, ill met by moonlight.

[Outro]
Fairies bless the beds, Puck sweeps the floor:
if we shadows have offended, call it a dream,
pardon us, and give us your hands!
Ill met by moonlight!
