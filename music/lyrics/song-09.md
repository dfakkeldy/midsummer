# Jack Gets His Jill

[Verse 1]
Let me tell it slow, love.
Theseus is counting four days
till he marries Hippolyta,
when Egeus walks his daughter in:
Hermia marries Demetrius,
or the law takes her life,
or she lives out her days as a nun.
She loves Lysander, so they whisper a plan:
tomorrow night, through the wood,
to his aunt's, beyond the law.

[Verse 2]
Now Helena loves Demetrius,
and he swore he loved her too,
till he saw Hermia. So she tells him,
hoping for one kind word.
He runs after them; she runs after him.
Same night, Quince's workmen,
Bottom the weaver as Pyramus,
slip out to rehearse a wedding play
where no one's watching.

[Chorus]
Jack gets his Jill, nothing goes ill,
every man gets his own again.
Jack gets his Jill, and all will be well,
but somebody's still under the spell.

[Verse 3]
In that wood a fairy king and queen
are fighting over a boy
she raises for his mother, her friend who died,
and the seasons come undone.
Oberon wants the boy. He sends Puck for a flower:
one drop on sleeping eyes,
you love the first live thing you see.
He hears Demetrius cast Helena off.
Puck, charm that man.

[Verse 4]
He drops it on his sleeping queen.
Puck sees Athenian clothes on Lysander
and takes him for the man.
Helena wakes him; he swears he loves her,
leaves Hermia sleeping in the dark.
Helena thinks he's mocking her, and goes;
Hermia wakes to no one.

[Chorus]
Jack gets his Jill, nothing goes ill,
every man gets his own again.
Jack gets his Jill, and all will be well,
but somebody's still under the spell.

[Verse 5]
Puck gives Bottom a donkey's head; his friends run.
Titania wakes, enchanted, loves him, has her fairies feed him honey.
Oberon catches the mistake and touches Demetrius' eyes himself.
Both men woo Helena; she calls it cruelty.
Hermia feels betrayed; the men go off to duel,
but Puck brings the fog, speaks in each man's voice,
and walks them round till all four lie down.

[Bridge]
Cure on Lysander's eyes, and only his.
Demetrius still wears the flower's kiss.
Oberon says the queen, while charmed, gave up the boy;
he frees her; Puck lifts the donkey head from Bottom's own,
and the fairies dance.

[Verse 6]
Dawn horns: Theseus finds the lovers and overrules Egeus;
three couples will wed, his own among them.
Lysander's back with Hermia;
Demetrius, still enchanted, loves Helena,
says some power turned his heart.
Bottom wakes, can't say his dream,
runs back to his crew.

[Verse 7]
At the feast, the couples watch the play:
lovers whisper through a Wall; a Lion stains Thisbe's cloak;
Pyramus thinks her dead,
stabs himself; she finds him, does the same. Just acting.
The couples laugh, the players dance,
and the fairies bless the house.

[Final Chorus]
Jack gets his Jill, nothing goes ill,
every man gets his own again.
Jack gets his Jill, and all will be well,
but somebody's still under the spell.

[Outro]
Puck says, if we offended, think it was a dream;
pardon us, and give us your hands.
Jack gets his Jill. Nothing goes ill?
