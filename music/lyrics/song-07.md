# Love-in-Idleness

[Verse 1]
I grow in a western field, far from the court
where Theseus counts four days to wed Hippolyta.
Egeus wants his Hermia to marry Demetrius,
or the law will take her life, or close her in a nun's cell.
She loves Lysander. They plan to run tomorrow night
through the wood, to his aunt's, beyond the law.

[Verse 2]
Helena loves Demetrius, but he loves Hermia now.
She tells him of the flight, hoping he'll be kind;
he follows them, she follows him into the trees.
And Quince's craftsmen come to rehearse a wedding play,
Bottom the weaver as Pyramus, Thisbe's love.

[Chorus]
Milk-white once, now purple with the wound,
love-in-idleness under the moon.
Close your eyes, and open soon,
love-in-idleness under the moon.

[Verse 3]
The fairy king and queen are quarreling
over a boy she raises for her dead friend's sake;
their war has drowned the fields and turned the seasons.
Oberon wants the boy. He sends Puck for me:
my juice on sleeping eyes will make you love
the first live creature that you see.
He hears Demetrius scorn Helena: charm that man.

[Verse 4]
Oberon presses me onto his sleeping queen.
Puck finds Athenian clothes, Lysander asleep,
and gives him me. The wrong man.
Helena wakes him; he swears he loves her,
leaves Hermia sleeping on the ground.
Helena thinks it's mockery and runs;
Hermia wakes, and the wood is empty.

[Chorus]
Milk-white once, now purple with the wound,
love-in-idleness under the moon.
Close your eyes, and open soon,
love-in-idleness under the moon.

[Verse 5]
Puck sets a donkey's head on Bottom; his friends flee.
The queen wakes with me in her eyes and loves him,
and her fairies serve him. Oberon sees the mistake
and lays me on Demetrius himself.
Now both men want Helena; she thinks they mock her.
Hermia feels betrayed. The rivals go to duel.

[Bridge]
Puck brings the fog and speaks in their voices,
walks them apart till all four sleep.
Another herb undoes me on Lysander's eyes,
only his.
Demetrius still wears my juice.

[Verse 6]
Oberon says the queen, still charmed, gave up the boy.
His herb undoes me in her eyes; Puck lifts the donkey head
from Bottom's own; the fairies dance in peace.
Theseus' horns wake the four; he overrules Egeus;
three couples will wed, his own among them.
Lysander, cured, loves Hermia again;
Demetrius, still mine, loves Helena,
and says some power changed his heart.
Bottom wakes, can't tell his dream, and finds his friends.

[Verse 7]
Married, three couples watch the workmen's play:
a Wall, then a Lion stains Thisbe's cloak; Pyramus thinks her dead,
stabs himself; she finds him, does the same. Only acting.
Bottom sits up; the court laughs; two players dance.

[Final Chorus]
Milk-white once, now purple with the wound,
love-in-idleness under the moon.
Close your eyes, and open soon,
love-in-idleness under the moon.

[Outro]
The fairies bless the house.
Puck says, if we offended, call it a dream,
and give us your hands.
Milk-white once, now purple with the wound.
