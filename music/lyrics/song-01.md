# First Thing You See

[Verse 1]
Four more days till the wedding bells,
Theseus and Hippolyta, Athens swells.
Egeus drags Hermia up to the throne:
wed Demetrius, the man I chose.
She loves Lysander. The duke is clear:
obey, or die, or a nun's cold years.
So they'll slip through the wood tomorrow night
to his aunt's, out of the law's sight.

[Verse 2]
Helena loves Demetrius; he loves her friend.
She tells him they're running, hoping he'll bend.
He races for the wood, she races behind.
And Quince's craftsmen have a play in mind:
Bottom the weaver will be Pyramus,
Thisbe's lover, for the wedding feast,
rehearsing by moonlight where nobody sees.

[Chorus]
Close your eyes, then open: the first thing you see,
that's who you'll love, that's who you'll be.
Moonlight, moonlight, nobody's free,
first thing you see, first thing you see.

[Verse 3]
Deep in the trees there's a fairy war,
Oberon and Titania fighting for
a boy she raises for his mother's sake,
her friend who died. Now the seasons break,
the rivers flood. Oberon wants the boy;
he sends Puck for a flower he knows:
drip it on sleeping eyes, and the sleeper goes
wild for the first live creature it sees.

[Verse 4]
He hears Demetrius drive Helena away:
Puck, charm that Athenian, turn his heart her way.
He drips the juice on his sleeping queen.
Puck spots Athenian clothes on a man in a dream.
Wrong man. Lysander wakes to Helena's face,
swears he loves her, leaves Hermia in that place.
Helena thinks it's a cruel joke and flees;
Hermia wakes up alone in the trees.

[Chorus]
Close your eyes, then open: the first thing you see,
that's who you'll love, that's who you'll be.
Moonlight, moonlight, nobody's free,
first thing you see, first thing you see.

[Verse 5]
Puck sets a donkey's head on Bottom's own;
the craftsmen scatter, he sings alone.
Titania wakes, charmed, loves the weaver on sight,
sends fairies to serve him all through the night.
Oberon sees the slip and charms Demetrius himself.
Now both men chase Helena: you're mocking me, she yells.
Hermia's furious, betrayed and confused,
and the rivals stalk off to find a place to duel.

[Bridge]
Puck brings the fog, borrows each man's voice,
leads them in circles till they've got no choice:
four asleep on the dew-wet ground.
The cure goes on Lysander's eyes alone.
Demetrius still wears the spell.

[Verse 6]
Oberon says the charmed queen gave up the boy.
He frees her eyes. Puck lifts the donkey head away,
Bottom's own face underneath. The fairies make peace.
Dawn horns: Theseus finds the four asleep,
overrules Egeus: you'll marry when I do.
Lysander's cured, and back with Hermia;
Demetrius, still enchanted, loves Helena.
Bottom wakes with a dream he can't explain
and runs to his crew, who cheer his name.

[Verse 7]
Married now, three couples watch the play:
a Wall, a Lion whose bloody mouth stains Thisbe's cloak.
Pyramus finds it, thinks she's dead, and stabs himself;
she finds him and does the same. Only actors.
The court heckles; Bottom sits up to offer a dance,
and midnight sends them all to bed.

[Final Chorus]
Close your eyes, then open: the first thing you see,
that's who you'll love, that's who you'll be.
Moonlight, moonlight, nobody's free,
first thing you see, first thing you see.

[Outro]
Fairies bless the house, Puck sweeps the door.
Puck says, if we offended, call it a dream;
pardon us, and we'll mend.
Give us your hands.
