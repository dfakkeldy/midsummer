# Bottom's Dream (It Has No Bottom)

[Verse 1]
Theseus would wed Hippolyta
in four days' time, they say,
when Egeus brought his daughter in
and swore she must obey:
wed Demetrius, or the law
would claim her life instead,
or a nun's cold cell. She loved Lysander,
so they planned to steal away
through the wood to his aunt's house,
beyond the law's long sway.

[Verse 2]
Now Helena loved Demetrius,
who'd sworn to her, then strayed.
She told him of the runaways,
just hoping he'd be kind;
he went chasing after Hermia,
and she ran close behind.
And Quince's crew went out to practice
by moonlight, out of sight,
a play of Pyramus and Thisbe,
Bottom in the lead, for the wedding night.

[Chorus]
It's Bottom's dream, it has no bottom,
nothing's the way it seemed.
The eye can't hear, the ear can't see,
it has no bottom, Bottom's dream.

[Verse 3]
Out in that wood the fairy king
and queen were bitter foes
over a boy whose mother died,
her friend, so the story goes.
Their quarrel flooded every field
and turned the seasons wrong.
Oberon wanted the boy, and sent
for a flower, small and strong:
a drop on sleeping eyelids
makes you love the first live thing you see.

[Verse 4]
He saw Demetrius scorn Helena
and told Puck, charm that man.
He charmed his queen; but Puck found Lysander
in Athenian clothes, and ran
the juice on the wrong man's eyes.
Lysander woke to Helena,
swore love, and left Hermia there.
Helena thought he mocked her;
Hermia woke to empty air.

[Chorus]
It's Bottom's dream, it has no bottom,
nothing's the way it seemed.
The eye can't hear, the ear can't see,
it has no bottom, Bottom's dream.

[Verse 5]
Then Puck gave Bottom a donkey's head,
and all his friends took flight;
Titania woke, enchanted,
and loved him at first sight,
had her fairies scratch his ears
and bring him honey sweet.
Oberon saw Puck's mistake
and charmed Demetrius in his sleep.

[Verse 6]
Now two men begged for Helena;
she thought it all a game.
Hermia felt robbed and cheated,
and the rivals' tempers flamed.
They went to duel, but Puck brought fog,
spoke in each rival's name,
and led them round till all four slept
before the morning came.

[Bridge]
He cured Lysander's eyes, only his;
Demetrius kept the spell.
Oberon told how the charmed queen gave up the boy;
he freed her eyes as well.
Puck lifted the donkey head away
from Bottom's own, and the fairies danced.

[Verse 7]
At dawn the horns of Theseus
woke the lovers where they lay;
he overruled old Egeus:
three couples would wed that day.
Lysander, cured, was Hermia's;
Demetrius, still charmed, loved Helena.
Bottom woke with a dream he couldn't tell,
and ran back to his friends.

[Verse 8]
That night they played for three wed pairs:
through a Wall the lovers sighed,
a Lion stained Thisbe's cloak,
Pyramus thought she'd died
and stabbed himself; she found him, did the same,
all acting, while the court joked;
then Bottom sat up and offered a dance.

[Final Chorus]
It's Bottom's dream, it has no bottom,
nothing's the way it seemed.
The eye can't hear, the ear can't see,
it has no bottom, Bottom's dream.

[Outro]
Fairies blessed the beds; Puck swept the floor:
if we shadows have offended,
call it a dream, and pardon us.
Give us your hands. It has no bottom.
